What you’ll do

Make a small change to the prototype and see how it appears in your browser. You’ll undo the change afterwards.

Steps

  1. In Visual Studio Code, use the Explorer on the left to open the src folder, then the app folder, then the views folder.
  2. Open a page you want to change. If you’re not sure which one, ask the person you’re working with.
  3. Change a word or a line of text.
  4. Press Ctrl+S to save your change.
  5. Go back to your browser. You should see your change. If you do not, refresh the page.
  6. In Visual Studio Code, press Ctrl+Z to undo the change, then Ctrl+S to save again.

What you should see

Your change should appear in the browser and disappear again after you undo it.

Information:

Do not delete or rename files unless the person who shared the prototype has told you to.

If something goes wrong

  • If your change does not appear, check that you saved the file. A dot on the file's tab means there are unsaved changes.
  • If the prototype does not load, check that it is still running in the terminal.
