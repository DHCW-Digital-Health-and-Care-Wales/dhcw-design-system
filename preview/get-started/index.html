Get started

Before building a service, teams often create prototypes to explore ideas, test user journeys and learn from users through research. Prototyping helps teams make informed design decisions and identify issues early. The design system supports both Figma and coded prototyping. This guidance will help you choose the most appropriate approach for your work.

Choose how you want to prototype

Figma prototypes

These are prototypes are created using the design tool Figma. They are useful for visualising what a service could look like and how users might move through it. Because they are quick to create and update, they are often used during the early stages of design.

Read how to set up Figma prototypes.

! Important: Getting access to Figma

Access to Figma for Digital Health and Care Wales staff is managed by the User-Centred Design team. The type of access you need will depend on how you plan to use Figma.

Email dhcw.ucd@wales.nhs.uk to discuss your requirements.

Coded prototypes

Coded prototypes are created using code rather than a design tool. They allow users to move through pages and complete tasks in a way that feels similar to using a real service. They are useful or understanding how journeys, forms and interactions may work before they are developed.

Read how to set up coded prototypes.

Accessibility and user research

The design system is built to support the design of accessible services. Components and patterns are reviewed against Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and refined through feedback.

Teams should still test their own services with users and make sure designs meet the needs of the people who will use them.

Get support and contribute

The design system is shaped by the feedback of teams across Digital Health and Care Wales. Help us improve the design system by suggesting changes and contributing patterns, components and guidance.

Email design.system@wales.nhs.uk to share your feedback.
